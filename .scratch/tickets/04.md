## Parent

Parent: #1 (Jumeau numérique immunitaire — Bilan Immuno Twin, MVP)

## What to build

Derive a score de réponse from the simulation: a continuous (non-binary) score that quantifies a patient's likely response to the applied immunotherapy, plus a flag for differential or unexpected response trajectories.

The score is deliberately continuous (not a hard classification) so a clinician can interpret with nuance. The CLI emits the score alongside the trajectories, and flags trajectories that diverge unexpectedly from a typical expected response. This directly serves the two clinical use-cases: predicting response pre-treatment, and surfacing differential responses.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A score de réponse (continuous, not binary) is derived from the simulation trajectories and output by the CLI alongside trajectories.
- [ ] Differential or unexpected response trajectories are flagged for the clinician.
- [ ] The score is interpretable and documented.
- [ ] Unit tests cover the score derivation; an integration test drives the score + flagging through the CLI.
- [ ] ADR-0004 respected (CLI only).
